I write a lot about what tools and models I'd *recommend*. This site is the other half of that: what actually happened when I tested them.

Every page here follows the same rule — state a hypothesis, run a controlled test, show the raw numbers, and write down what I'd actually do differently next time. No cherry-picked screenshots, no "it just works" claims. If a model or an idea underperformed, that gets published too.

## Why a separate site from my Decision Book

My [Decision Book](https://dev-mohammad.mintlify.app) answers "how should I think about this technical choice." It's framework and opinion.

This site answers a narrower, harder question: "what happened when I actually ran the test." It's evidence. The two are meant to be read together — one gives you the reasoning, the other gives you the receipts.
